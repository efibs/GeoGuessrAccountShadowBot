# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/efibs/GeoGuessrAccountShadowBot/security/advisories/new), not in a public issue.

Reports are most useful for anything that could:

- leak the `_ncfa` cookie or the Discord token,
- let someone without an authorized role act as the account,
- let text from GeoGuessr ping people or render as more than plain text in Discord.

If your `_ncfa` cookie may have leaked, log out of GeoGuessr everywhere you're able to, then log in again and use the new cookie.
